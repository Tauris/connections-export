Keep what your community built.

Capture HCL Connections wikis, blogs, forums, files & community front pages into a portable, self-documenting archive you control — with an offline reader and PDF export. Built for content you already have access to.

Keep what your community built

Years of pages, posts, and answers accumulate in these apps. Capture them in an open format you hold yourself, so the content isn't bound to the lifetime of any single platform.

A backup you can actually read

A lossless local capture with a built-in reader — browse pages, threads, and comments offline, and turn any of it into a PDF whenever you need a document.

Ready to move anywhere

The archive is an open interchange package: structure, metadata, attachments, and provenance stay machine-readable — far less information loss than a PDF — so content can be re-homed into whatever comes next.

More than a snapshot

Built-in exports and print-to-PDF give you snapshots — fine for a quick copy, but not made to be re-homed. A PDF flattens hierarchy, threads, versions, and comments; this capture keeps all of that intact.

Your own contributions, in context

Keep the discussions you were part of — everything you wrote, answered, or commented on, each with its full surrounding chain: the whole forum thread, the whole blog entry with every comment, never an isolated snippet. Name yourself in the “Only content authored by” field when you set up a capture and the sweep keeps just what you authored or took part in — matched on your name, user id, or contributor credit.

An archive from anywhere

Drop a folder or a .zip here — from your file manager, from OneDrive or SharePoint, or a link to one — and it opens in the Reader. A .zip is simply an archive folder compressed: zip a capture to email, move or store it, and open it here directly, read-only, without unpacking. It does not have to live in this folder, and nothing is copied when it is already on this machine.

Ingest defaults

PDF appearance

Renderer—

Sizes and colours used when rendering a PDF. Leave a field empty to keep the default shown in it. For anything not listed, connections-export style --dump writes the whole stylesheet out to edit.

Header and footer

Placeholders: {page}, {pages}, {title}, {date}, {section} — anything else is literal text. Empty means nothing is printed there. {section} names the wiki, blog or forum and the page you are on; where that cannot be known it falls back to the document title.

Archives

Type a path, or drag a folder onto this field. It must already exist — nothing is created here, so a typo cannot turn into an empty archives directory.

Stored—
Also settable with the CONNECTIONS_EXPORT_ARCHIVES_DIR environment variable (use . for the current directory). Defaults to ./connections-export-archives if unset.
